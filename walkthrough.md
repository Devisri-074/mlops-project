## 🚀 Key Interface Upgrades

1. **Executive Dashboard Platform Interactive Subscriber Viewer**:
   - **Click-to-View Subscribers**: Clicking any platform card (**Netflix**, **Prime Video**, **Spotify**, or **JioHotstar**) instantly scrolls to and filters the dedicated **Subscribers Directory & Viewer Table** right on the Dashboard.
   - **Detailed Subscriber Records**: Displays each subscriber's Avatar, Name, Email, Plan Tier & Pass (e.g. Premium 4K Annual), Device & Profiles, Weekly Consumption (Watch/Audio hrs), Monthly Fee, AI Churn Risk Badge, and Subscription Status.
   - **Quick Filter & Search**: Search subscribers in real time by name, email, plan, or device, and toggle status tabs (`All`, `Active`, `At-Risk`, `Churned`).
   - **One-Click Actions**: "+ Enroll [Platform] Subscriber" and "Open Full Directory" with URL query parameters (`/customers?platform=...`).
2. **Multi-Platform Data Seeding**:
   - Initial seed subscribers now populate across all 4 streaming services (Netflix, Prime Video, Spotify, and JioHotstar) with realistic subscription tiers, watch/listening hours, and risk profiles.
3. **Subscriber Enrollment (`/add-customer`)**:
   - Platform selection cards feature official vector logos, brand backdrops, and subscription tier descriptions.
4. **Streaming Analytics (`/analytics`)**:
   - Platform prediction simulator uses official vector logos with active glowing selection indicators.
5. **Subscriber Details (`/customers/:id`)**:
   - Header features the subscriber's platform official logo badge and ambient brand lighting.
